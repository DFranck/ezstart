## 🎯 Mission & Vision

### What (The Product)
EZStart is a **comprehensive web development ecosystem** that provides a curated suite of pre-built applications and tools, enabling developers to launch production-ready projects in days instead of months. It's a **monorepo-based platform** featuring authentication, payments, invoicing, AI-powered tools, and more.

### Why (The Problem)
- Building modern web applications from scratch takes **3-6 months minimum**
- Developers waste **60-80% of their time** on repetitive boilerplate (auth, payments, UI)
- Maintaining multiple separate tools is **expensive and time-consuming**
- No unified ecosystem exists for rapid SaaS development

### How (The Solution)
EZStart provides:
1. **Pre-built applications** (Auth, Payments, Invoicing, Games, AI Tools)
2. **Shared component library** (@ezstart/ui with 100+ components)
3. **Monorepo architecture** for easy customization
4. **Production-ready infrastructure** (TypeScript, tests, CI/CD)
5. **Open-source & free** to use and modify
