Example Use Case: Textile SME Success Story

1. Calculates carbon footprint → discovers high energy costs
2. AI recommends: solar panels + energy-efficient machines
3. Shows ROI: 30% energy cost reduction = $50K/year savings
4. Identifies: needs $200K loan for equipment
5. Returns to BIDV with clear business case
6. BIDV approves green loan (lower rate, preferential SBV refinancing)
7. SME implements → saves money → grows revenue
8. SME needs bigger working capital 2 years later → stays with BIDV
Result: BIDV gains loyal, growing, low-risk client + green loan assets
